The **Survey Details** tab is where you capture the written findings and supporting site information for a survey. It's organised into sections, each covering a different part of the survey. The same sections are available for every survey type.

To open it, go to the survey record and click the **Survey Details** tab.

<Tip>
  If you [copy a survey](/creating-survey-records#copying-a-survey) from a previous visit, everything on this tab is carried across. You only need to update what has changed since the last survey.
</Tip>

## Acknowledgement

A short acknowledgement thanking the client and site personnel for their time during the survey.

This section is pre-filled with standard boilerplate text. To change it, click **Edit Acknowledgement**, update the text in the editor, and save.

## Executive Summary

A summary of the survey — when and why the visit took place, the purpose of the survey, and the key outcomes.

Like the Acknowledgement, the Executive Summary starts with boilerplate text that you can tailor. Click **Edit Executive Summary** to update it.

## Positives

Positives record good practices and strengths observed at the site — for example, *Good layout for emergency response*.

To add a positive:

1. Click **New Positive**. A new row appears at the top of the table.
2. Describe the positive in the text editor.
3. Select a **Risk Category** to link the positive to the relevant risk classification element.
4. Click the tick to save, or the cross to cancel.

## Observations

Observations are areas of concern identified during the site inspection that haven't been raised as formal recommendations. They're short, simple notes on things the site should keep an eye on and is encouraged to consider for further action.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Name | A short title for the observation |
| Reference | An automatically generated reference (see below) |
| Risk Category | The risk classification element the observation relates to |
| Description | Details of the observation |

To add an observation, click **New Observation**, fill in the row, and click the tick to save.

<img src="/images/guide/survey-details-observations.png" alt="The Observations section on the Survey Details tab" />

### Observation references

Each observation is given a reference made up of **O**, the survey year, and a sequence number — for example, **O2026.04** is the fourth observation in a 2026 survey. The reference is filled in automatically, but you can edit it if needed.

### Observations vs recommendations

| | Observations | Recommendations |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Areas of concern for the site to keep an eye on | Formal improvement actions the business must take |
| Detail | Short and simple | Based on recognised codes and standards, industry practice, and loss control judgement |
| Prioritised | No | Yes — Critical (P1), Significant (P2), or Advisory (P3) |
| Tracked | No | Yes — reviewed regularly through to completion |

If an issue needs formal action and follow-up, raise it as a [recommendation](/creating-and-managing-recommendations) instead.

### Linking positives and observations to risk categories

Linking each positive and observation to a **Risk Category** — in the same way as recommendations — connects your findings to the survey's [risk classification](/risk-classifications-and-scoring). This allows findings to be analysed by risk element across surveys and sites.

## Loss History

A record of past losses and insurance claims at the site.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Date of Loss | The date the loss occurred (required) |
| Incident Details | A description of what happened |
| Amount Claimed | The amount claimed |
| Amount Paid | The amount paid out |
| Claims Status | **Claim Reported**, **Settlement in Progress**, **Settled**, **Declined**, or **Withdrawn** |

To add a loss, click **New Loss**, fill in the row, and click the tick to save.

<img src="/images/guide/loss-history-claims-status.png" alt="Adding a loss with the Claims Status options" />

## Major Updates

Significant changes or projects at the site — planned, in progress, or completed — such as new buildings, equipment upgrades, or process changes.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Description | What the update is |
| Value | The value of the project or change |
| Current Status | **Planned**, **In Progress**, **Completed**, or **Cancelled** |
| Start Date | When the update started or is due to start |

To add an update, click **New Major Update**, fill in the row, and click the tick to save.

<img src="/images/guide/major-updates-status.png" alt="Adding a major update with the Current Status options" />

## Utilities

A description of the site's utility supplies. The section starts with a standard list of utilities:

- Electricity
- Air
- Fuel
- Water
- Chiller
- Gas and Oil

Use the **`⋮`** menu on a row to edit the utility's name and description. You can also add new utilities or delete ones that don't apply to the site.

## Business Interruption

Information that helps assess how a loss would affect the site's operations and recovery. The section starts with a standard list of topics, including:

- Business Interruption
- Revenue / Profit Generation
- Production Lines, Key Plant Equipment
- Authorities, Licenses and Certifications
- Building Reconstruction Time
- Machinery Replacement Time
- Interdependencies within Organization
- Bottlenecks
- Suppliers
- Customers
- Warehousing
- Make-Up Capabilities
- Adequacy of Business Continuity Plan

Add a description for each topic that applies. As with Utilities, you can edit the labels and descriptions, add new topics, or delete ones that don't apply.

## Editing and deleting entries

Use the **`⋮`** menu at the end of any row to edit or delete it.

<Info>
  The Executive Summary, Positives, Observations, and Recommendations make up the [Closeout Report](/exporting-survey-reports).
</Info>
