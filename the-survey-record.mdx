---

The survey record is where engineers capture and manage all findings for a specific site visit — documents, recommendations, COPE data, loss estimates, and the audit trail. You land here after creating a survey or clicking one from the site's Surveys tab.

## Header

The top of the record shows the survey name and the site and client it belongs to ("for [Site] of [Client]"). A cloud icon after the site and client name shows that the survey is [available offline](/offline-surveys).

The following actions are available in the header:

- **Export** — download the survey as a **Survey Report** or **Closeout Report**. See [Exporting survey reports](/exporting-survey-reports).
- **Edit Survey** — update survey details such as status, visit date, type, or assigned engineer
- **`···`** menu — additional options:
  - **Copy Survey** — start a new survey for the site using this survey's data. See [Copying a survey](/creating-survey-records#copying-a-survey).
  - **Make available Offline** (or **Remove from offline mode**) — see [Working offline](/offline-surveys).
  - **Delete Survey**

## Tabs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Survey Overview">
    The default view. Shows:
    - **Documents** — files attached to this survey. Use **Add Document** to upload reports, photos, or supporting materials. Documents are shared with the client once the survey's status is **Report issued** and the client has portal access — see [Issuing a survey report](/issuing-a-survey-report).
    - **Recent Updates** — a live feed of changes to this record.
    - **Survey Details** — key survey information displayed in the right panel (see below).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Survey Details">
    The survey's written findings and site information — Acknowledgement, Executive Summary, Positives, Observations, Loss History, Major Updates, Utilities, and Business Interruption.

    See [Survey details](/survey-details) for more detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recommendations">
    All recommendations raised as part of this survey. Includes summary cards for Status, Priority, and Estimated Loss, plus a filterable table showing each recommendation's risk level, priority, loss estimate, and status.

    See [Creating and managing recommendations](/creating-and-managing-recommendations) for detail on creating and managing recommendations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="COPE">
    Construction, Occupancy, Protection, and Exposure data for the site. COPE captures the key physical and operational characteristics that underpin the risk assessment.

    See [COPE](/cope) for detail on completing COPE data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Loss Estimate">
    The site's total insured values and three loss scenarios (NLE, PML, and EML). See [Insured values and loss estimates](/insured-values-and-loss-estimates) for more detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Risk Classification">
    The survey's risk scores, including the Overall Score, a current-to-potential chart by risk element, and the Risk Elements table.

    See [Risk classifications and scoring](/risk-classifications-and-scoring) for more detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Change Log">
    A full audit trail of all changes made to this survey record.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Survey details panel

The right panel on Survey Overview surfaces key information at a glance.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Status | The current survey stage (e.g. Planning, Visit scheduled) |
| Visit Date | The date of the site visit |
| Assigned Engineer | The engineer responsible for the survey |
| Survey Company | The risk engineering company conducting the survey |
| Total Insured Value | The site's total insured value at the time of survey |

For a full explanation of survey statuses and what each stage means, see [Survey statuses](/overview-of-surveys-and-statuses).
