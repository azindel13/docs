---
title: "Issuing a survey report"
description: "The end-to-end workflow for finalising a survey and sharing the report with your client."
---

Once you've captured your findings in TruRisk, the final step is to produce the report and share it with the client. This page walks through that workflow from start to finish.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the survey in TruRisk">
    Fill in the survey's tabs — [Survey Details](/survey-details), [Creating and managing recommendations](/creating-and-managing-recommendations), [COPE](/cope), [Loss Estimate](/insured-values-and-loss-estimates), and [Risk Classification](/risk-classifications-and-scoring). This content is what populates the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the Survey Report">
    On the survey record, click **Export** and select **Survey Report**. The full survey downloads as a Word document, using the template for the survey type. See [Exporting survey reports](/exporting-survey-reports).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and finalise the report">
    Open the Word document, check it, and make any final changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the final report to the survey">
    On the survey record's **Survey Overview** tab, click **Add Document** and upload the finalised report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the survey status to Report issued">
    Click **Edit Survey** and change the **Status** to **Report issued**. See [Survey statuses](/overview-of-surveys-and-statuses).
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you set the status to **Report issued**:

- The survey is **locked** and can no longer be edited.
- The client admin is notified by email.

<Info>
  If you need to make changes after the report is issued, ask an Admin to change the survey's status. The survey can then be edited. Changing the status back doesn't send any notifications.
</Info>

## When the client can see the report

Documents uploaded to a survey become visible to the client in their Client Portal when **both** of the following are true:

- The survey's status is **Report issued**.
- The client has access to the Client Portal — one of their contacts has been promoted to client admin, which sets the client's status to **Active**. See [Contacts and Client Portal access](/contacts-and-client-portal-access).

<Warning>
  Anything in the survey's **Documents** table is shared with the client once the report is issued. Only upload documents you're happy for the client to see.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If this is the client's first issued survey, it's a good time to give them access to the Client Portal. See [Inviting a client to the Client Portal](/contacts-and-client-portal-access#inviting-a-client-to-the-client-portal), and use [Preview](/previewing-the-client-portal) to check what they'll see first.
</Tip>
