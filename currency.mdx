title: "Currency preference"
description: "Choose the currency TruRisk uses to display monetary values across the platform."
---

Monetary values in TruRisk — such as total insured values (TIVs), loss estimates, and costs to complete — are entered in the client's local currency. This is set on the client record when the client is created or edited, and is usually based on the client's locale (for example, US clients use USD).

You can view values in either of two ways:

- **The client's local currency** — values as they were entered for each client.
- **Your preferred currency** — all values converted into a single currency of your choice, so you can compare clients that use different currencies.

## Switching currency

Use the **currency button** in the top bar to switch the whole platform between each client's local currency and your preferred currency. The setting applies everywhere at once: every monetary value on every page — including TIVs, loss estimates and costs to complete — is shown in the currency you choose.

## Setting your preferred currency

Choose the currency used for conversions in your user profile. Click your name in the top-right corner, select **My Profile**, and set your **preferred currency**.

Your preference is saved to your user account, so it stays in place across sessions and devices. It only affects what you see — other users keep their own preferences.

## Exchange rates

Values are converted using exchange rates that are updated **once per day**. When a page shows converted values, it also shows the **source** and **date** of the exchange rates used. Conversion is for display only — the original values are never changed.
