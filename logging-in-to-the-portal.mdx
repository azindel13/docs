---
title: "Logging in to the portal"
description: "How to log in to the TruRisk portal with your username and password."
---

Once your account is set up, use your email address and password to log in to TruRisk.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the TruRisk portal">
    Navigate to your organisation's TruRisk URL in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter your email address and password, then click **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the portal">
    You'll be taken to your TruRisk dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot password** on the login screen to reset it.
</Tip>
