---
title: "Logging in to the portal"
description: "How to log in to the TruRisk portal with your username and password."
---

Once your account is set up, use your username and password to log in to TruRisk. Your username is almost always your email address.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the TruRisk portal">
    Go to [app.trurisk.io](https://app.trurisk.io) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter your username (usually your email address) and password, then click **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your verification code (if enabled)">
    If you've turned on multi-factor authentication, enter the code sent to you by SMS or email. See [Editing a user](/creating-and-managing-users#editing-a-user) to turn multi-factor authentication on or off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the portal">
    You'll be taken to your TruRisk dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot password** on the login screen to reset it.
</Tip>
