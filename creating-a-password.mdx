---
title: "Creating a password"
description: "Password requirements for your TruRisk account."
---

When setting up your account or resetting your password, your new password must meet the following requirements.

## Requirements

Your password must include:

- At least 8 characters (the more, the better)
- At least 1 uppercase letter
- At least 1 lowercase letter
- At least 1 number

<Info>
  Each requirement is validated as you type. All requirements must be met before you can submit.
</Info>
