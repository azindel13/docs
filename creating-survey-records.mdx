---
title: "Creating survey records"
description: "How to create a new survey for a site in TruRisk."
---

Surveys are always created at the site level. Each survey captures the findings from an engineer's site visit and drives the risk profile, recommendations, and reports for that site.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site record">
    Click **Clients** in the left navigation bar, select the client, then click the site you want to survey. You can also access surveys directly from **Surveys** in the left nav.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Surveys tab">
    Click the **Surveys** tab on the site record, then click **+ Add Survey**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete survey details">
    Fill in the survey information:

    | Field | Required | Notes |
    |-------|----------|-------|
    | Name | No | A descriptive name for this survey |
    | Company Name | No | Pre-filled with the parent client |
    | Site Name | No | Pre-filled with the site |
    | Status | No | The current stage of the survey. Defaults to **Planning** |
    | Visit Date | No | The date of the site visit |
    | Survey Company | No | The risk engineering company conducting the survey |
    | Type | Yes | **Property** or **Energy** — see below |
    | Assigned Engineer | No | The engineer responsible for this survey |

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Save Changes**. The survey record will open and the survey will appear under the site's Surveys tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A site can only have one open survey at a time. If the site already has a survey with any status other than **Report Issued**, you can't start a new survey for it — either from scratch or by copying — until that survey's report is issued.
</Warning>

## Copying a survey

When you return to a site you've surveyed before, you don't need to start from scratch. Copying a survey creates a new survey for the same site, pre-filled with the data from the previous one, so you only need to update what's changed since the last visit.

You can copy a survey in two ways:

- **From an existing survey** — click the **`···`** menu on the survey (in the Surveys list or the survey record header) and select **Copy Survey**.
- **When adding a survey** — if you create a new survey for a site that already has a survey, you'll be given the option to copy the existing one.

### What gets copied

All fields are copied to the new survey, with one exception: only **open** recommendations are carried across. Recommendations that were closed on the previous survey stay with that survey.

Open recommendations aren't duplicated — the same recommendation is linked to both the previous survey and the new one, so its full history is kept in one place.

## Survey types

The **Type** field determines which risk classifications and scoring categories are available within the survey, and which template is used when exporting the full Survey Report (see [Exporting survey reports](/exporting-survey-reports)).

| Type | Use for |
|------|---------|
| **Property** | Standard property risk surveys |
| **Energy** | Energy sector surveys |

<Info>
  Survey type cannot be changed after the survey is saved. Make sure to select the correct type before creating the record.
</Info>
