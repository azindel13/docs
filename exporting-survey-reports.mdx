You can export any survey as a Word document (.docx) from the survey record. There are two report types to choose from.

## Exporting a report

1. Open the survey record.
2. Click **Export** in the header.
3. Select **Survey Report** or **Closeout Report**.

The Word document downloads straight away. Both reports can be exported at any time, whatever the survey's status.

## Report types

| Report | Contents | Template |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Survey Report** | The full contents of the survey | Depends on the survey type — there is one template for **Property** surveys and one for **Energy** surveys |
| **Closeout Report** | A shorter report covering only the **Executive Summary**, **Positives**, **Observations**, and **Recommendations** | A single template used for all survey types |

<Tip>
  Use the **Closeout Report** when you need a concise summary of the key findings and actions, and the **Survey Report** when you need the complete record of the survey.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Export isn't available while you're [working offline](/offline-surveys).
</Info>
