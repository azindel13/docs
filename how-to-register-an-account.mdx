To register an account in TruRisk

Your TruRisk account is created by your account admin. Once they've added you, you'll receive an email invitation with instructions to complete your setup.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the relevant page">
    Navigate to the relevant section in the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the action">
    Follow your team workflow and save your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify results">
    Confirm the record or status updates as expected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">
    Check your inbox for an email from TruRisk. Click the button in the email to begin account setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a password">
    Enter a new password that meets the requirements. See [Creating a password](/creating-a-password) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    Review and accept the terms of service to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get started">
    You'll be taken to the TruRisk portal. Your account is ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If you haven't received your invitation email, check your spam folder or contact your account admin.
</Info>
