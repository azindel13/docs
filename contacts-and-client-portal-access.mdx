**Contacts** are the people at a client that you work with. They're stored on the **Contacts** tab of the client record (and of each site they're associated with).

Contacts don't have a login to TruRisk. They're a list for your own reference, and they're used for email notifications: when a contact is assigned to a recommendation, they receive an email whenever an engineer posts a response on it. See [The recommendation record](/the-recommendation-record#responses).

## Adding a contact

1. Open the client record and go to the **Contacts** tab.
2. Click **Add Contact**.
3. Enter the contact's details — name, **Job Title**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** — and select the **Site(s)** they're associated with.
4. Save the contact.

The **`···`** menu on each contact's row has the following options:

- **Promote Contact** — make the contact the client admin (see below)
- **Edit** — update the contact's details
- **Delete** — remove the contact

## Inviting a client to the Client Portal

Once you've completed and issued the client's first survey, promote their main contact to **client admin** to give the client access to the Client Portal. This turns the contact into a real user with client administration permissions — they can log in to view published survey documents, manage recommendations, invite other users, and more.

You can promote a contact in either of two ways:

- Open the **`···`** menu on the contact's row and select **Promote Contact**.
- Open the contact's **Edit** form and promote them from there.

A confirmation window asks you to **Enable portal access** for the contact. Click **Enable Access** to confirm.

Once promoted:

- The client's status changes to **Active** automatically.
- The contact receives an email inviting them to the Client Portal.
- A **crown icon** appears next to their name in the Contacts list to show they're the client admin.

<Info>
  You can only promote **one** contact per client. From then on, the client admin manages their own users from within the Client Portal — for example, adding site managers who complete activities at individual sites. If the client admin loses access or has problems with the portal, contact your TruRisk administrator.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Before you promote a contact, use **Preview** to check exactly what the client will see. See [Previewing the client portal](/previewing-the-client-portal).
</Tip>

The recommended order is:

1. Complete the client's first survey and [issue the report](/issuing-a-survey-report).
2. [Preview](/previewing-the-client-portal) the Client Portal.
3. Add the client's main contact and **Promote Contact** — portal access is enabled automatically.
