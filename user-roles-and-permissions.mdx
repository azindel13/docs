title: "User roles and permissions"
description: "An overview of the roles available to Risk Engineer portal users in TruRisk."
---

Every user in the TruRisk Risk Engineer portal is assigned a role that determines what they can access and do. Roles are assigned when a user is created and can be updated at any time by an Admin.

## Roles

There are two roles in the Risk Engineer portal:

**Risk Engineer** — The standard role for engineers who conduct surveys and manage client records. Risk Engineers can:

- Create and manage client and site records
- Create and manage surveys
- Raise and manage recommendations
- Schedule activities
- View reports and risk classifications

**Admin** — Admin permissions are given in addition to the Risk Engineer role (shown as *Risk Engineer, Admin* in the Users list). Admins can also:
- Create new users and manage existing users
- Create teams and assign users to teams
- Assign surveys to users outside their own teams
- Change a client's status (for example, disabling or reactivating portal access)
- Change the status of a survey that has been issued, so it can be edited

<Info>
  Only Admins can add new users and manage roles. If you need your role changed, contact your organisation's Admin.
</Info>

## How roles appear

A user's role is visible in the **Users** list under the **Role** column, and on the individual user record under **User Permissions**.

Role changes take effect immediately.
