---
title: "The navigation menu"
description: "An overview of the main navigation in the TruRisk Risk Engineer portal."
---

The TruRisk Risk Engineer portal uses a fixed sidebar on the left side of the screen for primary navigation, and a top bar that shows your current location on the platform (breadcrumbs) and provides access to search and your profile.

## Sidebar

The sidebar is always visible and provides one-click access to each main section of the platform:

| Section | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Dashboard with an overview of surveys in progress, recent recommendations, clients by score, recently visited clients, and recently updated surveys |
| **Clients** | Full list of client records, with filters and the option to add or import clients |
| **Surveys** | List of all surveys across all clients |
| **Recs** | List of all recommendations across all clients and surveys |
| **Activity Library** | Templates used for scheduling and managing activities |
| **Change Log** | A full audit trail of changes made across the platform |
| **Teams** | Manage teams and their members (admin only) |
| **Users** | Manage user accounts and roles (admin only) |

## Top bar

The top bar runs across the top of every page and contains:

- **Breadcrumb trail** — shows your current location in the platform (e.g. Clients → Hartmann Industrial GmbH → Dortmund Main Plant). Each segment is a clickable link so you can quickly navigate back up the hierarchy.
- **Search** — a global search bar for finding records across the platform by name.
- **User menu** — your name and avatar appear in the top-right corner - click here to change your personal preferences or logout.
