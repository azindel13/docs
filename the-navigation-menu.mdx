The TruRisk Risk Engineer portal uses a fixed sidebar on the left side of the screen for primary navigation, and a top bar that shows your current location and provides access to search and your account.

## Sidebar

The sidebar is always visible and provides one-click access to each main section of the platform:

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Home** | Dashboard with an overview of surveys in progress, recommendations by status, clients by score, recently visited clients, recently updated surveys, recent updates, and **Upcoming** — a list of recommendations that are coming due |
| **Clients** | Full list of client records, with filters and the option to add or import clients |
| **Surveys** | List of all surveys across all clients |
| **Recs** | List of all recommendations across all clients and surveys |
| **Activity Library** | Templates used for scheduling and managing activities |
| **Change Log** | A full audit trail of changes made across the platform |
| **Teams** | Manage teams and their members |
| **Users** | Manage user accounts and roles |

## Top bar

The top bar runs across the top of every page and contains:

- **Breadcrumb trail** — shows your current location in the platform (e.g. Clients → Hartmann Industrial GmbH → Dortmund Main Plant). Each segment is a clickable link so you can quickly navigate back up the hierarchy.
- **Offline Sync** — a cloud icon showing the sync status of your offline surveys. Click it to manage surveys available offline and view changes waiting to sync. See [Working offline](/offline-surveys).
- **Currency** — choose the currency used to display monetary values across the platform. See [Currency preference](/currency).
- **Search** — a global search bar for finding records across the platform by name.
- **User menu** — click your name in the top-right corner to open:
  - **My Profile** — update your name, phone number, and profile photo, change your password, turn on multi-factor authentication, and manage email notifications
  - **Logout** — sign out of TruRisk
