---
title: "Previewing the client portal"
description: "See exactly what a client will see in their TruRisk Client Portal before you share access."
---

**Preview** lets you see exactly what a client will see in their TruRisk Client Portal — their dashboards, recommendations, surveys, and sites — before you give them access. Use it to check that the client's risk profile is complete and presented the way you want before you give them access by promoting a contact to client admin.

## Starting a preview

You can start a preview from either of two places:

- **The client record** — click the **`···`** menu in the header and select **Preview**.
- **The Clients list** — click the **`···`** menu at the end of the client's row and select **Preview**.

The client portal opens in a new browser tab.

<Info>
  Preview is available for every client, whatever their status — including **Pending** clients who can't access the portal yet.
</Info>

## During a preview

A banner at the top of the page confirms that you're previewing the client portal for that client, and shows how long is left in the preview session. You're signed in to the portal as a **Visitor**.

You can move around the portal using its sidebar, just as the client would.

- **Everything is read-only.** You can view the client's information, but you can't make any changes.
- **Some pages aren't available.** Certain pages, such as the Change Log, show a message that the Visitor user isn't authorised to view them.

## Ending a preview

To return to the engineer portal at any time, click **Back to engineer portal** in the banner.

A preview session lasts **1 hour**. When it expires, you're returned to the engineer portal automatically. You can start a new preview session whenever you like.
